# Lucky — "Hey Lucky" voice assistant for Android

Say **"Hey Lucky"**, wait for the beep, then speak:
- "Set an alarm at 5 AM" → sets a real alarm in your Clock app
- "Wake me up at 6:30" / "Timer for 10 minutes"
- Any question → answered by Gemini (free key) and spoken aloud

The wake word is detected **offline on the phone**. Nothing is sent anywhere until you say "Hey Lucky".

## Install
1. Open **Releases** → latest → download **Lucky.apk** on your phone.
2. Open it. If asked, allow "Install unknown apps" for Chrome/Files.
3. In Lucky: paste your Gemini key → Save, tap the 3 permission buttons, then **Start "Hey Lucky"**.
